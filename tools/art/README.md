# Original portrait atlas

`animal_portraits.png` contains the six cropped source portraits in a 3 by 2 grid. The asset generator converts them to the GBA sprite palettes and renders the player’s unlockable cosmetics.

The built-in image generator used this prompt.

> One production 3-column by 2-row sprite atlas for an original Game Boy Advance billiards game. Square canvas divided into six precisely equal cells, isolated bust centered per cell, generous transparent gutters, black or dark ink contours, true transparent background. In strict reading order: upper left a cheerful river otter in teal striped waistcoat (round snout), upper center a cool penguin in navy vest and bow tie, upper right a confident red fox in orange silk shirt, lower left a sly octopus with plum skin and turquoise collar, lower center a champion great white shark in a teal dinner jacket and gold lapel, lower right a human player with dark hair, tan face, teal bowling shirt. Charming expressive anthropomorphic character portraits with high contrast and bold simple shapes recognizable at 64 pixels, premium 32-bit hand-pixeled sprite look. Big clear eyes, memorable shapes and compact color clusters, no grain or dither, crisp edges. Original character designs. No lettering, props, frames, text, background, brand, shadows outside silhouette. Every subject must stay inside its cell.

## Finn's portrait

`finn_portrait.png` is the built-in image generator's redraw of Finn. The sprite generator fits it to the roster's height and uses a dedicated 16-color palette to reproduce the red fabric shading in both portrait sizes.

> Redraw this exact original anthropomorphic great white shark pool champion as a polished Game Boy Advance pixel-art portrait with a red tuxedo and matching red bow tie. Draw the jacket in clear warm scarlet, brick-red shadow clusters and restrained lighter red highlights, with shading that follows the shoulders, sleeves and lapels. Keep dark crisp linework, a white dress shirt, the small gold laurel pin, the blue-gray shark skin, friendly mischievous smile, three-quarter pose, and the narrower neck at the viewer-left collar. Draw a taller full bust with both shoulders fully extended and sloping naturally into the jacket. Keep the face large and clear at 56 and 16 pixels. Use broad deliberate pixel-art clusters and a tight palette. Center the bust with transparent padding on all sides. The game draws the red halo separately, so the artwork itself must contain only the shark and transparent background.

## Wardrobe portraits

`wardrobe_portraits.png` holds a 4 by 2 atlas. The built-in image generator used the original player portrait as the identity reference. The sprite generator also recolors the fitted vest’s shirt to warm linen and the bow tie’s shirt to a cool dress shirt.

> Image 1 is the identity, pose, and pixel-art style reference. Produce ONE RGBA Game Boy Advance asset atlas, strict 4 columns by 2 rows even cells, eight isolated copies of that same tan young adult pool player (same swept black hair, same brows, turn toward the right, teal bowling shirt, same eye-level and shoulders). Transparent empty gutters, no background, frames, fonts, logos, labels or extra objects. Each portrait must stay wholly within its cell. In reading order: (1) dark forest-green finely striped buttoned vest worn over shirt, fitted with gold trim; (2) frosted navy satin bow tie properly around the neck under both shirt collar points; (3) burnt-orange fox ears on a dark unobtrusive headband, nestled in the hair; (4) plum baseball cap fitting the skull with a curved visor following his three-quarter angle, small brass badge; (5) fitted steel-blue headband with a single rear-swept shark fin above the crown; (6) dark smoked sunglasses worn properly over both eyes with arms passing to the ears, smaller lens on far side, nose bridge and pale reflected glints; (7) teal competition cloth headband naturally tucked under the hair fringe, small square embroidered patch; (8) golden sports visor with hollow top, gold band snug against forehead, turned brim projects forward in the direction of his face and casts a subtle skin shadow. One accessory per cell. Keep a consistent character across cells, matching reference design. Hand-pixeled GBA portrait style, bold outlines, good broad highlight and shadow clusters, crisp stair-step edges with no dithering or noise. Full isolated busts, scale and positioning uniform, fully transparent backdrop.
